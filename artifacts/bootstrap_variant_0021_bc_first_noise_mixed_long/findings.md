## Interpretation and scope

The hypothesis (0015 with twice the budget) gets a negative answer: the extra 2,000 updates yield a single-checkpoint deterministic peak at 2,500 that is gone by 3,000, as in 0007's unreplicated 1,000 peak (wave-1 synthesis); the 5-trial stochastic never exceeds 2/5, below 0004's 3/5, and the 30-trial rate rises to 43 % at 4,000 only while the mean trajectory fails by palm support. From online 1,000 on the handoff mean sits within L2 .014–.059 of the label (it was .082 at 500), so the flicker is not first-control error; the latent std (.157 at 4,000, peak .163 at 3,000) stays within the wave-1 range .13–.20, while alpha (.000463) exceeds the wave-1 range .00036–.00038, consistent with the synthesis's open stochastic-ceiling question. Prefix-8 retention held (deterministic pass at all points, ≥25/30); the prefix-7 30-trial rate fluctuated throughout (11/30 at 1,000, 8/30 at 3,000, 15/30 with a deterministic drop at 4,000), so no association with the actor-LR ramp is supported here; 0020, which also ran at full actor LR, is the comparison for that.

Single seed, first half a replay of 0015. All 288 training successes are assisted, demonstration-mixed, changing-policy episodes; none is primitive evidence and no trajectory was exported. Cold trials failed everywhere (0/14 stage, 0/270 large-sample stochastic, 0/9 large-sample deterministic), so no checkpoint is deployment-eligible; frozen claims apply only to the listed checkpoints and seeds.
