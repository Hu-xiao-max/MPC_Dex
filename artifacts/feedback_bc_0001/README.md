# Supervised feedback learner around the spine

`train.py` trains an archived-format actor (configurable hidden sizes) by mean-only regression on the spine rows (x10) and the MPC correction rows of the tube collections (`mpc_spine_tube_0001/{tracking,tracking2,dagger_round*}`), holding out every branch whose root perturbation direction is 3 (second-order children inherit their parent's direction), then evaluates: regression error on the held-out correction rows (a chaos-free generalisation measure), closed-loop deterministic/stochastic trials from the cold reset and every spine prefix, and deterministic handoffs at the first corrected state of training and held-out branches (cells at perturbed index <= 9 are the informative ones; later cells are open-loop tolerance). The split is frozen in `runs/<tag>/manifest.json`. `dagger_round.sh R` trains on everything collected so far and branches from the learner's own rollouts into `dagger_round(R+1)` (`mpc_spine_tube_0001/dagger_tube.py`, tracking planner). No critic, no RL; nothing here is primitive-eligible, and a checkpoint produced here carries untrained critics and no environment contract metadata.

Results so far (2026-10-06, first-order tube only, 121 training / 41 held-out branches): 256x256 fits the training rows to L2 0.015 but generalises poorly (held-out regression median 1.34) and completes 1 of 20 informative corrected-state handoffs; 1024x3 memorises the rows (1e-4), generalises better but still poorly (0.80), completes 7 of 20 training and 1 of 19 held-out informative handoffs and spine prefixes 3-5 deterministically; cold reset 0 in both. Capacity matters; the coverage of the perturbation space (9 directions in 16 dimensions per state) is the limit, hence DAgger at the learner's own states.
