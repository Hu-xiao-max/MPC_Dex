## Interpretation and scope

The hypothesis (0015's design replicated at a fresh seed) is reproduced only in kind: both seeds show isolated deterministic prefix-6 passes (0015 at 250, 750, 1,250; here at 500, 1,500), a stochastic ceiling of 2/5, and a final checkpoint at 2/30–4/30, versus 20/30 for 0004 and 0/30–19/30 across 0019's points. Handoff noise did not widen the basin around recorded control 7 in either seed, consistent with the wave-1 finding that sigma .025 peaks do not replicate. Prefix-7 retention in this seed is the weakest of the family (large-sample prefix 7 ≤ 21/30 after pretraining, versus 26/30 for 0004 at 1,000 and 2,000 and 28/30 for 0015 at 500 and 2,000); because the same noise design at the other seed retained prefix 7 and the no-noise mixed-prefix stage 0019 also dipped (14/30 at 250, 19/30 at 750), the deficit is not attributable to noise from one seed per cell. The handoff mean stays within .02–.06 of the label from 250 on while latent std doubles, so the failures are not first-control mean error.

Single fresh seed. All 170 training successes are assisted, demonstration-mixed and collected under a changing policy; none is primitive evidence and no trajectory was exported. Cold trials fail at every point, so no checkpoint is deployment-eligible.
