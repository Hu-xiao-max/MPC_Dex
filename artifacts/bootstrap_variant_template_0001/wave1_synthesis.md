## Factor attribution

- **MPC demonstration rows present vs absent** (confidence 0.85): Removing the MPC rows is the factor that changes the outcome in kind: it stops the actor mean drifting toward the unfinishable MPC continuation, removes the hold-to-horizon mode, and is present in every stage with any frozen prefix-6 pass or late prefix-7 retention. It is not sufficient: 0002 never passes deterministically at prefix 6 and cold remains 0/9.
  Evidence: Single-factor, seed-matched pair SAC0004 (MPC kept) vs 0002 (removed): online prefix-6 training successes 2/94 vs 64/441; handoff L2 to recorded control 7 at online 2,000 1.242 vs .043; frozen prefix-6 stochastic 0/5 at every point vs 1/5 at 500 and 2,000 (same seed); prefix-7 deterministic at 2,000 trunc vs pass (stoch 0/5 vs 2/5). Across the family: the five stages keeping MPC rows (SAC0004, 0001, 0011, 0012, 0013) have 0 frozen prefix-6 deterministic passes, prefix-7 retention 0/6 at online 2,000 in all five, and 11–22 horizon-truncated frozen trials each; the ten stages without them have 0 truncated frozen trials, 9/10 pass the deterministic prefix-7 trial at 2,000 (0007 is the exception), and 7/10 show at least one deterministic prefix-6 pass (0003, 0004, 0006, 0007, 0008, 0010, 0014). 0002 alone (no other factor) already shows the handoff mean moving onto the recorded control and partial prefix-7 recovery.
- **Actor BC toward recorded rows: all rows vs first row vs none** (confidence 0.7): First-row BC is the best single factor in wave 1 for prefix-6 stochastic success and full retention; all-row BC saturates the mean but yields no stochastic prefix-6 pass at fixed prefix 6 (0/20 in 0003), consistent with supervision on rows 8–9 constraining the inherited continuation. The deterministic pass is checkpoint-dependent (0017 fails at 2,000), so the ceiling is 3/5 stochastic, not a solved handoff.
  Evidence: Fixed prefix 6, no MPC, seed-matched triple: 0002 (none) deterministic prefix-6 0/3 online checkpoints, best stochastic 1/5, training 64/441, prefix-7 stochastic at 2,000 2/5; 0003 (λ10 all rows) deterministic 1/3 (500 only), stochastic 0/20, training 34/454, prefix-7 stochastic 3/5; 0004 (λ10 first row) deterministic 2/3 (1,000 and 2,000), stochastic 3/5 at both, training 109/432, prefix-7 and prefix-8 6/6 at 1,000 and 2,000. All three put the mean within L2 .02–.04 of recorded control 7 by 500, so the difference is not the first-control mean. All-row BC with mixed prefixes (0005, 0010) gave deterministic passes 0/3 and 1/3 and stochastic ≤2/5. The wave-2 replicate 0017 (verified from its own evaluation reports, outside the assigned findings) shows deterministic prefix-6 passes at 4 of 8 checkpoints, palm support at 2,000, stochastic 3/5 at 2,000 and 4/5 at 1,750, prefix-7/8 6/6 at 2,000.
- **Mixed collection prefixes {6,7,8}** (confidence 0.55): Mixing prefixes did not prevent prefix-7 forgetting when MPC rows were kept and was not necessary for retention once they were removed; it supplies many prefix-7/8 changing-policy successes but no measurable prefix-6 gain (single seed per cell, spread within seed noise).
  Evidence: With MPC rows kept, 0001 vs SAC0004: prefix-6 training 0/40, no frozen prefix-6 success, prefix-7 lost at online 500 and 0/6 at every later point (same as SAC0004); only prefix 8, whose training episodes kept succeeding (20/25), was retained. With no MPC and BC all rows, 0005/0010 vs 0003: prefix-7 deterministic pass at every point in all three; stochastic prefix-7 at 2,000 2/5, 5/5 vs 3/5; prefix-6 deterministic 0/3, 1/3 vs 1/3; stochastic best 2/5, 1/5 vs 0/5. 0006 (prefixes {7,8} only) held 12/12 prefix-7/8 trials at every point without any prefix-6 stream. Retention without mixing was already held by 0003 and 0004 (deterministic prefix-7 pass at 2,000).
- **Handoff noise sigma (.025 vs .05)** (confidence 0.4): σ .025 coincided with the only 5/5 stochastic prefix-6 point in wave 1, but it was a single-checkpoint, single-seed peak that fell to 1/5 within 1,000 updates and did not recur in the replicate; σ .05 degrades the inherited suffix before any learning and never masters the perturbed neighbourhood. Noise is not shown to widen the basin durably.
  Evidence: 0007 (σ .025, no BC, mixed, no MPC): deterministic prefix-6 pass at 500, 1,000 and 2,000; stochastic 0/5, 5/5, 1/5; handoff L2 .113 → .046 → .090 with std .076 → .103 → .199; perturbed prefix-7 trials 0/5 at 2,000 and perturbed prefix-8 4/5; perturbed training success 32/150 (prefix 7) and 99/165 (prefix 8). Its seed replicate 0018 (wave 2, recounted from its reports) gives deterministic prefix-6 passes only at 750 and 1,250, stochastic 0/5 at 1,000, best 2/5 at 1,250, 1/5 at 2,000, cold 0/14. σ .05 (0008, 0009): perturbed prefix-7/8 baseline trials 1/10 vs 10/10 unperturbed in both seeds; warm-up perturbed successes 5/71 and 31/80 (0008), 10/80 and 24/82 (0009); noisy frozen trials never above 1/5 at any point in either seed; prefix-8 clean stochastic at 2,000 2/5 in 0008. 0007 differs from 0001 in two factors (noise and MPC rows), so wave 1 does not isolate the noise.
- **Online demonstration share .05 vs .3** (confidence 0.75): A smaller online share does not stabilise the actor; it lets the prefix-6 failure rows dominate sooner and lets the critic forget the demonstration values. The hypothesis that 9.6x row repetition drives the drift is not supported at this seed.
  Evidence: 0011 vs SAC0004 (single factor, seed-matched, pretraining identical record-for-record): online prefix-6 training 0/162 vs 2/94; handoff latent std at online 500 1.037 vs .213; L2 to the recorded label at 500 3.007 vs 1.781; recorded-row critic bias at 2,000 −.711/−.715 vs −.235/−.219; prefix-7 retention lost at 500 in both; 13 frozen truncations. In the dart recipe (share .05 with BC all), recorded-row bias during online training −.57 to −.78 (0008) and −.62 to −.75 (0009), against −.15 to −.48 in the share-.3 BC variants (0003, 0004, 0005, 0010).
- **Actor/temperature losses excluding demonstration rows (actor mask)** (confidence 0.7): Actor gradients on demonstration states are not the driver of the drift; the excursion comes from the on-policy prefix-6 failure rows (std stays near baseline only when that stream is absent, as in 0006). Masking alone changes nothing in kind.
  Evidence: 0012 vs SAC0004 (single factor, seed-matched; every online update used 179 on-policy actor rows and 77 critic demonstration rows): prefix-6 training 0/130 vs 2/94; no frozen prefix-6 success (0/24); prefix-7 lost at 500 in both (0/18 online); handoff std at 500 .781 vs .213; L2 to MPC label at 500 2.930 vs 2.006. 0006 (mask plus BC all plus prefixes {7,8}, no prefix-6 stream): std stayed .061–.083, mean within .007–.010 of the label, prefix-7/8 12/12 at every point, prefix-6 deterministic pass/drop/pass, stochastic 1/20.
- **Seed replicates (0013 vs SAC0004; 0010 vs 0005; 0009 vs 0008; wave-2 0017 vs 0004 and 0018 vs 0007)** (confidence 0.8): Seed-to-seed spread within a design is about one deterministic pass and two of five stochastic trials per cell, and single-checkpoint peaks do not replicate; design-level differences that exceed this (MPC removal, first-row BC stochastic 3/5 at 2,000 in both seeds) survive, checkpoint-level claims (which checkpoint passes deterministically) do not.
  Evidence: 0013 vs SAC0004: outcome in kind reproduced (no deterministic prefix-6 pass, prefix-7 6/6 → 0/6 at 500, hold-to-horizon at 1,000 and 2,000); differences of 1/5 stochastic prefix-6 and prefix-7 at 1,000 and training 1/85 vs 2/94. 0010 vs 0005: deterministic prefix-6 1/3 (2,000) vs 0/3; stochastic best 1/5 vs 2/5; prefix-7 stochastic at 2,000 5/5 vs 2/5; training prefix-6 44/239 vs 32/241. 0009 vs 0008: deterministic 0/3 vs 1/3; stochastic best 3/5 vs 1/5; prefix-7 stochastic at 2,000 3/5 vs 1/5; prefix-8 5/5 vs 2/5. 0017 vs 0004 (recounted here): stochastic 3/5 at 2,000 in both, deterministic fails at 2,000 in 0017 (passes at 500, 750, 1,500, 1,750). 0018 vs 0007: 5/5 at 1,000 not reproduced (0/5); best 2/5.
- **Full reverse curriculum (prefixes 0–8, 9 recorded rows, BC all)** (confidence 0.8): The full curriculum produced no cold or early-prefix competence at this budget; its prefix-6/7/8 results are within what 0004, 0006 and 0010 achieved with 3 rows, and the 9-row buffer left the critic uncalibrated on the demonstrations.
  Evidence: 0014: prefixes 1–4 0/96 frozen trials and 0/207 training episodes; prefix 5 one stochastic pass (17612 at 1,000) and 4/45 training; prefix 6 deterministic pass plus 3/5 at 500, 1/6 at 1,000, 0/6 at 2,000; prefix-7/8 6/6 at 2,000; cold 0/24 frozen (drops moved from control 1 to control 3, peak .93 rad at 500) and 0/88 training (40 warm-up, 48 online). Critic never fit the 9 rows (bias −.83/−.81 after pretraining, −.71/−.61 at 2,000, versus about −.06 on the 3 recorded rows in the other no-MPC stages); latent std at obs0 collapsed .053 → .0066 while the mean sat .112 from recorded control 1.

## Open questions

- Basin versus knife-edge at obs6: every BC variant places the deterministic mean within L2 .01–.06 of recorded control 7, yet deterministic trials from that state pass at some checkpoints and drop at others (0003, 0006, 0008, 0014, and 0017 at 2,000). No artifact measures the state the policy's own control 7 produces or how far it lies from recorded state 7, so state deviation versus later-control error is undecided.
- Stochastic ceiling: with the mean on the label, stochastic prefix-6 success never exceeds 3/5 except the unreplicated 0007 peak; the handoff latent std grows from .067 to .13–.20 in every variant with a prefix-6 stream and alpha rises monotonically (.000306 → .00036–.00038) in all 14 stages. Whether a fixed or lower temperature, or a std held at the 0006 level (.06–.08), lifts the ceiling is untested.
- Handoff noise is not isolated in wave 1: 0007 differs from 0001 in noise and MPC rows; the wave-2 pair 0019 (bc_first_mixed) versus 0015 (bc_first_noise_mixed) was stamped for this and has completed (status complete_sac_stage) but is not part of this synthesis and was not verified here beyond the 0017/0018 frozen tables.
- Demonstration-row critic bias re-drifts negative after pretraining in every BC variant (−.15 to −.48 at share .3, −.57 to −.78 at share .05, −.71 in 0014) even though the rows are in every batch; the mechanism (on-policy prefix-6 failures dragging the values, or the entropy-free pretraining target versus the SAC online target) and its effect on the actor are unmeasured.
- Prefix-7 stochastic retention in mixed-prefix BC-all runs diverges by seed (0005 5/5 → 2/5, 0010 19/20 across points) and prefix-8 stochastic dips at online 500 recur in 0001, 0003, 0005, 0011, 0013, 0014 before recovering; whether this early transient has a common cause is unknown.
- No variant shows any cold competence (0/9 per stage, 0/24 in 0014; 0/88 cold training episodes in 0014). In 0014 the mean at obs0 sits .112 from recorded control 1 with std .0066 and cold drops moved from control 1 to control 3; whether the prefix-0 continuation is reachable at all by this actor, given the critic never fit the 9-row buffer (bias −.83 after pretraining), is open.
- 0006's held-out prefix-6 critic bias rose to about +1.0 after pretraining with no prefix-6 stream; whether the same overvaluation appears in a chained stage that inherits a checkpoint and moves the handoff earlier (wave-3 0024/0025, completed but not synthesized here) is unchecked.
- Correction for the supplied 0006 findings: its second frozen table lists online-1,000 prefix-6 deterministic as 'Drop, 3 controls' and cold deterministic as 'Drop, 2 controls'; the per-trial reports give 6 controls (final 4.97 rad, max 5.46) and 5 controls, matching its first table. No findings.md exists yet in any of the 14 variant directories; the supplied texts should be written with the first table's values.
- All wave-2 and wave-3 stages (0015–0025) report status complete_sac_stage in their summary.json; only 0017 and 0018 frozen counts were recounted here to check replicate claims. Their findings and the claim in prepare.py that 0004's checkpoint carries 'prefix-7/8 retention 5/5' (it is 6/6 including the deterministic trial) remain for a separate synthesis.
