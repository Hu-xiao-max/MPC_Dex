# Frozen checkpoint evaluation; large-sample stochastic success rates

Read-only evaluation of saved variant checkpoints (`artifacts/bootstrap_variant_*/checkpoints/<label>/agent.pt`) with the stage's own archived runner: for prefixes 5, 6, 7, 8 and cold reset, one deterministic trial plus 30 stochastic trials with fresh seeds (23000 + 100 * prefix + i). Physics, prefix replay checks, the force-qualified success criterion and the 100-control horizon are those of the variant runner. Learner tensors are hash-checked unchanged after every checkpoint. Per-trial physics and transitions are not stored; per-trial report fields are. Results are in `results/<variant>/<checkpoint>.json`; `targets.txt` lists what was evaluated; `summary.md` is produced by `summarize.py`. No learning occurs and nothing enters any replay; these are frozen-policy estimates with 30-trial binomial uncertainty, not deployment evidence (cold reset remains the deployment criterion).
