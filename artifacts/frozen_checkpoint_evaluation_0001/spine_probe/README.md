# Spine probe

`probe_spine.py <variant> <checkpoint> <K> <demo...>` evaluates a frozen checkpoint from every prefix p = 0..n-1 of the named demonstrations (1 deterministic trial, seed 42, plus K stochastic trials with seeds 60000 + 100 p + i). Result: `spine_probe_0082_online_4000.json` and `probe_0082.log` (K = 3) over the eight demonstrations of the deepest shared groups (seeds 132, 140, 193, 194 share their first three controls; 119, 141, 155, 157 the first two with them). Used on 2026-10-05 to choose the spine of stage `bootstrap_variant_0084_ms_spine193_sarsa_from82`: seed_193 (14 controls) is the only candidate the `0082` online_4000 policy completes from its last four prefixes deterministically and 3/3 stochastically (p10-p13); seed_157 from p14 of 19; the others only intermittently. Selection criterion: the longest contiguous run of deterministic passes ending at the last control, ties broken by shorter length.

## Open-loop basins along the spine (2026-10-05)

`basin_spine.py <variant> <ckpt> <demo> <j_from> <j_to> <radii> <trials> <tail_k>` perturbs control j of the demonstration by a random direction of L2 radius r and continues in three ways: `hold` replays the remaining recorded controls open-loop and then repeats the last one (at most 20 extra) until the boundary; `tail` replays open-loop up to control tail_k - 1 and lets the frozen policy finish; `policy` lets the frozen policy continue immediately. Result for seed_193 with the `0084` online_1000 policy (`basin_193.log`, `basin_seed_193_0084_online_1000.json`, 4 trials per cell): r = 0 succeeds 4/4 in every mode and at every control; r = 0.003 fails 0/4 open-loop at controls 0-7 and the policy fails 0/4 at controls 0-8; tolerance appears only where the remaining horizon is short (control 9: r = 0.003 3/4; control 10: r = 0.01 4/4, r = 0.03 4/4 open-loop; controls 11-13: 4/4 at every radius). The policy's success tracks the open-loop tolerance (never better, at control 9-10 slightly worse), so the policy's competence at the end of trajectories is open-loop tolerance of the last few controls, not feedback; the demonstrations are chaotic as open-loop sequences and the MPC succeeds by re-planning from the true state every control. A separate check (diag of 2026-10-05) found r = 0.0003 at control 0 fails as well, with the state diverging only after 9-10 controls at a contact event.
