The saved 25-command probe was replayed exactly: all recorded joint/object positions and velocities match with zero numerical difference, as do every recorded palm-force sample. Independently summed world contact forces match MuJoCo's free-object constraint force to 2.5e-14 N.

Holding for a requested additional 2 s exposes a limitation: the strict environment drops the object at **2.9 s**, only **1.0 s after the completed turn**. Success termination was disabled for this check; palm and drop criteria stayed enabled. A separate postmortem observation continues physics to 3.9 s with the failure latched, solely to cover the full requested observation window. Those post-failure states are not demonstration or training data. Thus the branch proves a supported full turn, not sustained rotation or stable terminal holding.
