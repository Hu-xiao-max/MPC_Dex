# Companion simulation-SAC prerequisite: source audit

The cached sources establish the prerequisite's origin and extraction procedure, but do **not** publish its SAC training recipe. This audit separates the companion planner (arXiv:2609.14868v1) from the requested main RL method (arXiv:2609.14878v1). No training, physics, source, or configuration was changed. File identities and exact locators are in `findings.json` and `manifest.json`.

## What the companion actually specifies

- Train a **SAC policy in simulation on a 40 mm across-flats hexagonal object, without domain randomization**; execute that trained policy to obtain coordinated finger trajectories. Its direct physical deployment failed, so hardware transfer is not a prerequisite for extracting the simulation prior. [Companion III-B1, paragraph 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14868v1#S3.SS2.SSS1.p3.1).
- The companion project implementation table adds **five extraction trajectories** and the exact parenthetical **“5 trajectories, ~1 min”**. It does **not** say whether the minute is aggregate or per trajectory. Treat five as an explicit collection target; retain the duration as ambiguous rather than converting it into five one-minute episodes or a 60-second total requirement. The heading/table has no HTML ID: locator is **Implementation Details → Rotation primitive source**, cached `artifacts/sources/planner-project.html:396`. [Companion project](https://primitive-informed-mpc.github.io/).
- The companion does not specify turns per extraction trajectory or whether each trajectory begins at a reset. Those details cannot be imported from the main method's twenty one-turn hardware demonstrations.
- Temporally align the joint trajectories, subtract their mean, and apply PCA to their covariance. Retain the smallest component count reaching **90% variance**; the authors obtained **four** rotation components. Four is a reported outcome, not an instruction to force every new dataset to rank four. [Companion II-D](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14868v1#S2.SS4.p2.1), [III-B1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14868v1#S3.SS2.SSS1.p3.1).
- The rotation task holds the object **without palm support**, rotates about its principal axis, and counts a full revolution without a drop. Table contact is a rollout failure. These are explicit task/MPC statements; the companion does not give the simulation SAC environment's precise termination implementation or numeric retention thresholds. [Task/success](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14868v1#S3.SS2.SSS1.p1.2), [no palm support](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14868v1#S3.SS2.SSS1.p2.1), [table constraint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14868v1#S3.SS2.SSS1.p4.1).

## Settings that belong to a different stage

The main paper defines normalized 16-dimensional joint-position actions, the observation terms, 10 Hz control, and the reward `20*progress − .3 + 200*success`, replaced by −50 on drop. Its project table specifies 60 inputs, 256×256 tanh/LayerNorm networks, ten critics, 12k mean-BC and 50k Soft-SARSA updates, std .3 after BC, batch256, online UTD8, and a 900-step horizon. These are **main real-world learning** settings, not a published companion-SAC recipe. [Main interface](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14878v1#S3.SS1.SSS0.Px2.p2.2), [reward](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14878v1#S3.SS1.SSS0.Px2.p3.2), [main project, Implementation Details → Real-world SAC with MPC scaffolding](https://real-world-rl-with-mpc.github.io/).

Likewise, epsilon .02 clips demonstration actions for the main **BC latent target**; it is not a published physical command restriction to ±.98. The PPO comparison alone uses 192 environments, 15M transitions, action EMA .5, domain randomization and advantage normalization. It is neither the companion SAC primitive policy nor evidence for observation normalization in SAC. [Main PPO comparison](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14878v1#S3.SS2.SSS2.p1.1), main-project table at cached `project.html:302–329`.

The main paper reports that its pretrained policy achieved about 140° on average and zero full turns in ten trials. Therefore, failure of standalone BC to complete a turn is **not** itself a contradiction of the main method. It remains distinct from obtaining the successful simulation-SAC behavior needed for the companion prior. [Main III-B1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14878v1#S3.SS2.SSS1.p3.1).

## Recommended prerequisite audit target

Freeze a demonstrably successful simulation SAC checkpoint on the 40 mm task; collect five policy-only trajectories with actual observations/actions/joint states, independent force audits, checkpoint identity, and individual durations. Document any local preprocessing, reward, control, reset and training choices without attributing unknown settings to the companion. Preserve episode boundaries for the alignment/PCA decision. The current `configs/paper_reference.json` already records the geometry/no-randomization/PCA requirements and normalization uncertainty, but omits the companion project's five-trajectory and ambiguous-duration detail; it was not edited by this audit. Current MPC, BC, and refined-head diagnostics remain useful local evidence, not a substitute for that SAC provenance.
