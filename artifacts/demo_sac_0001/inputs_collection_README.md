# Cold-reset MPC demonstrations with the diagnostic prior

`collect.py <seed_start> <seed_end>` runs the corrected native MPC (archived recipe and frozen source of `mpc_prefix6_handoff_recovery_0001`: eight workers, 350 candidates, ten elites, three iterations, six knots over .5 s, .1 s controls, retained progress, perpendicular weight 100, .98 search bound, projected reference candidates) from the cold reset (physical seed 42) with one CEM seed per attempt and at most 40 controls. A strict success (environment success, force-qualified full turn, at least two loaded fingers, no palm, table or other support, no drop, no equality constraints) is re-executed from a fresh cold reset with a separately recomputed force audit; only a bit-for-bit match is stored under `episodes/seed_<n>/` (transitions with qpos/qvel/targets/integration states, 2 ms physics samples, report). Every attempt is logged in `attempts_<start>_<end>.jsonl`.

The prior is the measured 40 mm diagnostic PCA prior, not the paper's SAC-derived primitive; the main-pipeline `collect`/`pretrain` gates reject it, so these demonstrations are inputs for artifact-level stages only and are not primitive-eligible. Feasibility: `../mpc_cold_feasibility_0001/` (read-only probe, six CEM seeds).
