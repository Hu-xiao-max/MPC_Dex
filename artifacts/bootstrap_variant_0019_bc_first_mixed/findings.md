## Interpretation and scope

The hypothesis was to isolate the contribution of σ .025 handoff noise in `bc_first_noise_mixed` (0015, same seeds). Recounted from 0015's own reports: prefix-6 deterministic passes at 250, 750, 1,250, stochastic best 2/5 (1,250) and 1/5 at 2,000, large-sample best 11/30 (1,250) and 4/30 at 2,000, training 22/167. Without noise (this stage) the handoff is reached more often mid-run (19/30 at 1,750, 81/254 training) but not at the end (0/30). At these seeds the noise did not add prefix-6 competence; neither design holds it to the final checkpoint. Against 0004 (fixed prefix 6, same BC; large-sample 16/30 at 1,000 and 20/30 at 2,000 with deterministic passes), mixing prefixes 7/8 gave no measurable prefix-6 gain and coincided with the terminal collapse, consistent with the wave-1 attribution that mixed prefixes are neither necessary for prefix-7/8 retention nor a prefix-6 gain. The collapse at 2,000 coincides with the handoff mean moving to .094 from the label and the demonstration critic's first-row value halving; whether it is a transient like the 750/1,000 dips or durable cannot be told from one checkpoint and one seed. The 1,750 checkpoint (deterministic pass, 19/30, prefix-7/8 29/30 each) is this run's best point, but the final checkpoint is the worst prefix-6 point of the run.
