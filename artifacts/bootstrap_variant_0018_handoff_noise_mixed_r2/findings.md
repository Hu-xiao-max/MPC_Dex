## Interpretation and scope

The hypothesis under test was whether 0007's online-1000 peak (prefix-6 5/5) recurs under new seeds. It does not: 0/5 at online 1,000, and the run's best prefix-6 point is 2/5 at 1,250. What replicates is the design's outcome in kind as the wave-1 synthesis characterised it (deterministic passes at some checkpoints, no stable stochastic basin, prefix-8 retained, cold 0): 0007 passed deterministically at 500/1,000/2,000 with stochastic 0/5, 5/5, 1/5; 0018 passes at 750/1,250 with 0/5, 2/5, and 1/5 at 2,000. Without a BC term the handoff mean settles only within L2 .06–.10 of recorded control 7 (0007: .046–.113) while the latent std more than doubles, and the perturbed prefix-7 neighbourhood that σ .025 was meant to master stays unmastered by the frozen policy (3/45 noisy prefix-7 trials; 0007 noisy prefix-7 0/5 at 2,000), so the noise is not shown to widen the basin durably. This matches the synthesis's seed-replicate estimate (spread of about one deterministic pass and two stochastic trials per cell; single-checkpoint peaks do not replicate) and confirms that the 0007 online_1000 checkpoint should not be inherited on the strength of its peak.
