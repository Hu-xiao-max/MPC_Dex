# `cylinder_first_control_0001`: how precisely must a policy reproduce the first controls of a cylinder demonstration?

Read-only diagnostic on the verified cylinder demonstrations (`cylinder_demonstrations_0001`, 40 mm cylinder, archived period and grasp, strict force audit), run in the environment and with the runner of stage `bootstrap_variant_0099_cyl_spine172_from96` (its `run.py` is imported for `load_sources`, `Episode`, `environment_for_policy`; nothing in that stage is modified). Motivation: stage `0099` pinned the actor to seed_172's recorded controls at every state of that demonstration to within L2 0.02-0.04 and still failed every cold-reset trial, while the campaign's frontier on seed_172 sits at the last four to five of its eight controls.

Scripts (each writes only into this directory):

- `bc_fit_172.py [start_checkpoint] [lr] [steps]`: fine-tunes the actor of `0096` online_4000 by behaviour cloning alone (MSE in atanh space, the runner's label clip) on seed_172's eight recorded rows, prints the per-row L2 between the actor's deterministic action and the recorded control, then rolls the fitted actor out deterministically from the cold reset and from the states after one, two and three recorded controls, and ten times from the cold reset with the actor's own latent std. Writes `bc_fit_172.json` and the fitted actor `actor_bcfit_172.pt` (scratch weights; not a stage checkpoint).
- `diag_172.py`: with the fitted actor, three rollouts from the cold reset in the same environment: exact open-loop replay of the recorded controls; the actor fed the recorded observations (its actions then differ from the recorded controls only by the fit residual); the closed loop. Per step it logs the L2 distance of the live observation from the recorded one and of the executed action from the recorded control. Also the actor's gain |da|/|dobs| for random observation perturbations of radius 1e-5 to 1e-2 at the recorded states. Writes `diag_172.json`.
- `bifurcation_172.py <source ...>`: open-loop tolerance of the first control at radii 1e-6, 1e-5, 1e-4, 3e-4, 1e-3: the first control is perturbed by a random direction of that L2 length (ten directions per radius, fixed seeds), the remaining recorded controls are replayed, success is the stage's force-qualified criterion; also the L2 deviation of the observation after that one control and, for one 1e-5 perturbation, the six observation components that deviate most. Writes `bifurcation_<sources>.json`.
- `mpc_action_noise.py <sigma> <seed_from> <seed_to> [max_controls]`: the collector's recipe (`cylinder_demonstrations_0001/collect_used.py`: archived CEM, planner sees the true state, re-plans every control) with Gaussian noise of std sigma per dimension added to every executed control (clipped to the search bound); one JSON row per attempt appended to `attempts_sigma<sigma>.jsonl` (strict force-qualified success, controls, failure, final angle, mean L2 of executed minus planned control). Uses this directory's `native_cache` (a copy of `control_period_0001/native_cache`).
- `collector_check.py <seed ...>`: runs `collect_used.run_attempt` unchanged for the given seeds (cross-check that the noise script at sigma 0 reproduces the collector; nothing is stored).
- `gain_172.py`: read-only re-measurement, from the saved weights, of the console-only numbers of sections 1-2 (start and fitted per-row residuals, fitted BC MSE and latent std, actor gain under random observation perturbations); `gain_172.json`.
- `spine_residual_0099.py [variant] [demo]`: per checkpoint of a stage, L2 between the actor's deterministic action and the recorded control at each state of one demonstration; `spine_residual_0099_seed_172.json`.

Results and reading: `findings.md`. Scratch weights `actor_bcfit_172.pt` (sha256 42b18397...) are the BC-fitted actor of section 1, not a stage checkpoint and not eligible for anything.
