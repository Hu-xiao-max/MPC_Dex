# `cylinder_first_control_0001`: findings

Environment: the archived 40 mm task with `object_shape: circular`, period 0.1 s, the stage runner of `bootstrap_variant_0099_cyl_spine172_from96` (strict force-qualified success, the same criterion the stages use). Demonstrations: `cylinder_demonstrations_0001` (cylinder) and `mpc_cold_demonstrations_0001` (hexagon, through the runner of `bootstrap_variant_0084_ms_spine193_sarsa_from82`). All numbers are read from the JSON files in this directory (`gain_172.py` re-measures the start and fitted residuals, the fitted actor's BC MSE and latent std and the actor gain from the saved weights: `gain_172.json`), except the step count, wall time and the mid-training BC MSE of section 1, which come from the console output of `bc_fit_172.py` and are not stored.

## 1. Behaviour cloning alone reproduces seed_172's controls to 1e-5 at the recorded states (`bc_fit_172.py`, `bc_fit_172.json`)

Starting from the `0096` online_4000 actor (L2 to the recorded control 1.699, 0.132, 0.151, 0.026, 0.121, 0.035, 0.039, 0.018 at seed_172's eight states), 2,000 Adam steps (lr 1e-4; the script's early stop at the first multiple of 2,000 steps with every row below 5e-5) of the runner's BC loss on the eight rows alone bring every row to L2 at most 1e-5 (saved actor: 1e-6 to 7e-6 per row, BC MSE 3.8e-12 in atanh space, latent std 0.0098-0.0101) in 2 s. The eight-row fit is therefore not limited by the network's capacity: stage `0099`, whose spine BC on the same eight rows shares the actor with 50 other sources' rows, the SAC actor gradient, a shared-state term and a log-std anchor, kept a residual of 0.02-0.07 at these states from online_500 to online_3500 and 0.15 at the cold reset at online_4000 (`spine_residual_0099_seed_172.json`); which of those other terms sets that residual is not isolated here.

Rollouts of the fitted actor in the stage environment: deterministic from the cold reset: fail, palm support after all 8 controls, force-qualified angle 5.215 rad; from the state after one recorded control: fail, palm support after 6 controls, 5.444 rad; after two: success (6 controls); after three: success (5 controls). Ten stochastic cold trials with the actor's own latent std (0.01): 0/10, five by palm support and five by a drop, after 3-6 controls.

## 2. Action errors of 2e-6 to 6e-6 at every control are already too large (`diag_172.py`, `diag_172.json`)

Three rollouts from the cold reset: exact open-loop replay of the recorded controls succeeds (6.687 rad) with zero deviation at every step. The fitted actor fed the recorded observations (so its executed controls differ from the recorded ones only by the fit residual, 5.6e-6, 5.3e-6, 3.9e-6, 1.7e-6, 5.9e-6 at controls 0-4) drops the object at the fifth control: the live observation deviates from the recorded one by 3.1e-3 after control 0, 0.20 after control 1, 0.69 after control 2 and 3.97 after control 3. The closed loop (actor fed the live observation) executes all eight controls and fails by palm support at 5.215 rad; its action deviation grows 5.6e-6, 6.1e-3, 0.32, 1.00, 1.27, 2.64, 3.73, 3.39 while the observation deviation grows 3.1e-3, 0.20, 2.11, 5.70, 7.29, 17.2, 18.7. The actor's gain |da|/|dobs| at the recorded states is median 0.9-1.05 (max 14-20) for perturbation radii 1e-5 to 1e-2 (`gain_172.json`): the closed loop does not correct; the rollout through the actor at the recorded observations already diverges at the same rate through control 2, and the closed loop runs ahead of it from control 3 (2.11 against 0.69 at t = 3).

The exact replay (r = 0) succeeds for every demonstration (logged for ten in the two `.log` files; seed_172's in `diag_172.json`). Of the eleven, two (seed_116 and the hexagon seed_193) keep 7 of 10 at r = 1e-5 and lose at 1e-4; seed_172 is at 4-5 of 10 at r = 1e-6 and 1e-5 and 2-3 of 10 from 1e-4; the other eight are at 0-3 of 10 already at r = 1e-6. The observation one control later deviates by about 8e-6 per 1e-6 of perturbation on the sources that share the majority first control (seeds 101, 102, 153, 155, 116 start with the same control, so their deviations coincide), i.e. a continuous one-step amplification of 6-17 (up to 80 at r = 1e-3) from the first control to the next observation (later controls were not measured one by one; the multi-step record in section 2 grows 3.1e-3, 0.20, 0.69, 3.97), and by 3e-3 in 7 of 10 directions for seed_172 (1.5e-2 to 1.7e-2 in 7 of 10 for seed_154, 2e-4 to 8e-4 for seed_174), i.e. a discrete, direction-dependent jump whose origin was not logged.

**Reading.** The probed demonstrations are open-loop knife edges from their first control: a first-control error of 1e-5 still reproduces seed_116 and seed_193 in 7 of 10 directions and seed_172 in 4 of 10, at 1e-4 and above no source exceeds 2 of 10, and errors at every control (section 2) fail at 6e-6; the fit residual of a shared actor is 0.02-0.07 (stage `0099`; `feedback_bc_0001`'s floor 0.013). The suffix curriculum's frontier on each source (in `0099`: the last three to six controls on seed_172, the last two to three on seed_116, the last one to three on seed_174, the last one on seed_153 and none on seeds 154 and 155) is where the remaining amplification of such an error stays below the success margin; walking it back further along a recorded trajectory cannot work on any of the eleven probed sources, whichever of them is used as the spine. What a learned policy would have to supply instead is closed-loop correction of its own errors at every control, which the recorded trajectories do not demonstrate (the native MPC supplies it by re-planning with lookahead from the true state). Section 4 tests whether a controller with that imprecision can complete the turn at all.

## 4. The native MPC completes the turn with executed-action noise of the size of a learned actor's error (`mpc_action_noise.py`, `attempts_sigma*.jsonl`, `collector_check.py`)

The archived collector recipe (planner sees the true state, re-plans every 0.1 s with a 0.5 s horizon, 350 samples, 10 elites, 3 iterations), unchanged except that each executed control is the planned one plus Gaussian noise of std sigma per dimension, clipped to the search bound. Cross-check: with sigma 0 the script reproduces the collector's own `run_attempt` exactly (seed 300: no feasible plan at the second control, 1.391 rad; seed 301: 40-control budget exhausted at 1.645 rad in both). Strict force-qualified successes over planner seeds 300-311 (300-303 for sigma 0.003):

| sigma per dimension | executed minus planned, median over attempts of the per-attempt mean L2 per control | strict successes | controls of the successes | failures |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | 9/12 | 6, 7, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 16, 21 | no feasible plan at control 2; budget (40 controls) twice |
| 0.003 | 0.011 | 2/4 | 9, 15 | no feasible plan at control 2; budget |
| 0.01 | 0.035 | 7/12 | 9, 9, 10, 14, 14, 15, 16 | no feasible plan at controls 2, 2, 4, 12, 28 |
| 0.03 | 0.101 | 7/12 | 9, 9, 16, 16, 17, 18, 18 | no feasible plan at controls 2, 2, 2, 5, 15 |

Seed 300 fails at the second control at every sigma up to 0.01 (its first plan leads to a state with no feasible candidate among 1,050), a planner-seed property. With per-control execution errors of L2 0.035 (sigma 0.01), 35 times the largest radius of section 3's table and 3,500-35,000 times the first control's measured tolerance (1e-5 to 1e-6), the re-planning controller still completes 7 of 12 turns, and 7 of 12 at 0.101 (the collector's own sigma-0 rate on its 64 attempted seeds in 100-195 was 54 of 64).

**Reading.** A controller whose executed controls are off by 0.03-0.10 per step can complete the cylinder turn from the cold reset, provided it re-plans from the true state with lookahead at every control. A per-control execution error of that magnitude does not by itself prevent a re-planning controller from completing the turn (the test adds independent random errors to a controller that re-plans from the true state; it does not test a systematic, state-correlated residual without re-planning). What separates the learned actors from this controller is that they imitate recorded open-loop trajectories whose first-control tolerance is 1e-5 to 1e-6 (section 3) and carry no corrective law. This is the closed-loop counterpart of the hexagon finding in `mpc_spine_tube_0001` and `feedback_bc_0001` (per-step slack 0.005-0.01 for a tracking law around one spine against a learner floor of 0.013): the slack exists for a re-planning controller that may leave the spine, not for a tracker of a recorded trajectory. The learned-policy route this suggests is supervision by the re-planning planner's corrections at the policy's own visited states (DAgger with the native planner as the expert; the noise-perturbed executions of this section, DART-style, are an untested alternative on the expert's own trajectory). The hexagon work found the planner's labels at the archived 0.5 s horizon to be sampling noise, pinned only by a longer horizon (1.0-1.5 s) with a tracking penalty; on a policy's own rollout there is no reference to track, so the label-noise question is open on the cylinder. Nothing here is a frozen-policy result; no primitive-eligible data was produced; the circular object remains a disclosed deviation.
