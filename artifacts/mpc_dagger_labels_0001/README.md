# DAgger-style MPC labels at policy-visited states

`label.py <policy_variant> <checkpoint> <demo> ...` takes held-out demonstrations the frozen policy fails on, replays each demonstration up to its last `s` controls, lets the policy act deterministically (recording its controls and failure), and for branch points `b` (0, 1, 2 policy controls kept) hands the episode to the archived corrected native MPC (diagnostic prior, eight workers, at most 20 corrections). Strict successes are re-executed from a fresh cold reset with a separate force audit and stored only on a bit-exact match, in the demonstration layout plus a per-control `control_origin` array (`demo_prefix`, `policy`, `mpc`). Each stored episode is a full cold-reset trajectory whose prefix (demonstration controls plus policy controls) is physically replayable, so it can serve as a multi-source prefix source whose suffix contains the MPC corrections at states the policy actually visited. Nothing here is primitive-eligible.

Result (2026-10-02, policy `bootstrap_variant_0079_ms_suffix1to8_weighted_from74` online_4000, demonstrations seed_194/195/196, suffixes 1-6, branches 0-3): 48 branches attempted, 28 stored (17 from the demonstration state itself, 11 from states reached after 1-3 policy controls); 12 branches had no feasible MPC plan because the policy's control had already released the object. Details in `findings.md` (generated by `summarize.py`), per-branch records in `attempts_*.jsonl`, state diagnostics in `diag/policy_branch_states.json`. Consumed by `bootstrap_variant_0080_ms_dagger_from79` (template `extra_sources`).
