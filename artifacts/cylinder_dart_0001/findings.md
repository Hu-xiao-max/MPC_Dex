# `cylinder_dart_0001`: findings

All numbers are read from the JSON/NPZ files in this directory (`consistency.py` prints per-step medians and maxima; the per-pair values quoted below are recomputed from the NPZ arrays). Planner configuration throughout sections 1-3: the archived collector recipe, 0.5 s horizon (5 controls), 350 samples, 10 elites, 3 iterations, diagnostic PCA prior; the "persistent" planner carries its shifted elite mean from one control to the next, a "fresh" planner is reset at the current controls (sampling centred on the hold of the current targets, spread at the recipe's floors, no history). The archived `ProjectedReferenceCEM` injects the hold of the current targets as a candidate, so a label equal to the previously executed control is that hold candidate.

## 1. Four DART rollouts of one planner seed (`dart_rollout.py`, `consistency.py`)

Planner seed 302 (a strict success without noise in `cylinder_first_control_0001`, 16 controls), execution noise std 0.01 per dimension (executed minus label, mean L2 per control 0.032-0.038), four noise seeds: strict successes in 11 (n1), 13 (n3) and 14 (n4) controls and a 40-control budget failure at -0.42 rad (n2). The four share the cold reset and the same first label. After one control their observations are 0.34-0.59 apart (last-action part 0.042-0.057, the executed noise) and their second labels split two ways: n2, n3 and n4 return the hold of the executed first control (0.046-0.056 apart, each within 0.03-0.04 of its own first label) and n1 a rotation 1.92-1.93 from them. After two controls n1 is 13.5-13.6 from the other three, which are still 0.38-0.60 apart, yet their third labels are 2.2-3.1 apart in every pair; after three controls all six pairs are 17.0-22.1 apart and from then on 9.9-27.4 (per-step medians 14.8-20.1), with labels 1.4-4.5 apart (per-step medians 2.2-3.6). Within a rollout the label moves by 1.5-4.4 from one control to the next in n1, n3 and n4 (n4 once by 0.05), while in the failed n2 it equals the previously executed control at 31 of 39 transitions and moves by at most 0.05 at 38 of them: the persistent planner re-issued the hold candidate for 38 controls and never rotated the object.

## 2. Fresh planners at the same state (`label_spread.py`)

Along the persistent planner's own closed loop (seed 302 without noise, 16 controls, strict success; seed 301 with noise 0.01, 17 controls, then no feasible plan), three fresh planners with other CEM seeds were run at each visited state. Where they do not coincide with the persistent control they lie 1.4-4.0 (seed 302) and 1.0-3.9 (seed 301) from it (medians 2.50 and 2.66) and 0.6-4.1 from each other. All three coincide with it at 1 of 16 steps (seed 302, t = 11) and 1 of 17 (seed 301, t = 1); one or two of them do at t = 0 and t = 1 (seed 302) and t = 0 (seed 301); at 3 and 2 further steps the three fresh planners return one identical control that is 1.6-3.4 from the persistent one. The disagreements are of the size of the controls themselves (label norms 0.9-3.8). The number of feasible candidates per step was not recorded, so the coincidences are not attributed to a cause here.

## 3. Recovery from the `0099` policy's own states (`recover_from_policy.py`)

The `0099` online_3500 actor drops the object at its third control from the cold reset. Handing the planner the state after its first control: strict success in 15 planner controls (the planner's first control there is 2.72 from the policy's own); after its second control: no feasible plan among 1,050 candidates; after its third: already terminated. The `0096` online_4000 actor also drops at its third control; after its first control the planner reached the environment's success condition in 10 controls without passing the strict audit (6.08 rad force-qualified); its second control lies outside the planner's action search bounds, so that handoff was not run.

**Reading.** In the tested configuration the re-planning planner's control at a state is not a function of the state: at observations 0.34-0.60 apart it returns the hold or a rotation 1.9 away (section 1, steps 1-2), fresh planners at one state disagree by 1-4 (section 2), and noise-displaced copies of one closed loop become unrelated trajectories within two to three controls (section 1). Its tolerance to execution noise (`cylinder_first_control_0001` section 4) therefore comes from re-planning after every step, not from a stable state-feedback law. A DAgger or DART regression on these labels would have no single-valued target at the states that matter; whether a regressor fitted to them would still produce usable controls was not tested here (the hexagon precedent, `demo_sac_0001`, is the mean of different demonstrations' first controls, which failed). The planner can take over from the state the `0099` actor reaches after one control, a possible hybrid but not a frozen learned policy. Within this configuration (0.5 s horizon, the archived sampling budget) the closed-loop imitation route is blocked by set-valued labels, as the open-loop route is by the 1e-5 to 1e-6 first-control tolerance; section 4 tests a 1.0 s horizon with the archived sampling floors and finds it cannot plan at all. Nothing here is primitive-eligible; the circular object is a disclosed deviation.

## 4. A 1.0 s horizon with the archived sampling floors never leaves the hold (`label_spread.py`, `DART_HORIZON_CONTROLS=10`)

The same probe with the planning horizon doubled to 10 controls (1.0 s) and everything else unchanged (seed 302, no noise): at all 40 controls the persistent planner and the three fresh planners return the identical hold of the current targets (label norm 0.91, every distance 0), the object is never rotated (-0.44 rad after 40 controls, budget failure). With the recipe's per-knot proposal floors unchanged, the longer horizon leaves the hold as the only feasible candidate, the effect `control_period_0001` found for longer control periods; a longer-horizon expert would need re-tuned floors (and, on the hexagon, a tracking penalty to be deterministic, `mpc_spine_tube_0001`), which is a planner-design question outside this probe. `label_spread_s302_sigma0_h10.json`.
