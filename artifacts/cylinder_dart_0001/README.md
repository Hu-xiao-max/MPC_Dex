# `cylinder_dart_0001`: is the re-planning planner's control a learnable function of the state?

Read-only probes on the 40 mm cylinder (archived collector environment and planner of `cylinder_demonstrations_0001/collect_used.py`, period 0.1 s, 0.5 s horizon, 350 samples, 10 elites, 3 iterations, diagnostic PCA prior, strict force-qualified success), motivated by `cylinder_first_control_0001` section 4: the re-planning planner completes the turn under 0.035-0.10 of execution noise, so a policy that imitated it at its own visited states (DAgger/DART) looked like the remaining learned-policy route. These probes measure whether such labels exist as a function of the state.

- `common.py`: the collector's environment, planner construction (`planner_for`: a planner reset at the current controls, i.e. sampling centred on their hold with the recipe's floor spread and no history; horizon in controls from `DART_HORIZON_CONTROLS`, default the archived 5), one planner call (`plan_once`) and the frozen-actor loader (repository `SACAgent`, the stage evaluations' inference contract; the stage runner cannot be imported next to the planner modules).
- `label_spread.py <planner_seed> <sigma> [fresh=3]`: one closed-loop rollout of the persistent warm-started planner; before each executed control, three fresh planners (other CEM seeds, no warm start) are run once at the same state; records the L2 distances between their controls and the persistent one. `label_spread_s302_sigma0.json`, `label_spread_s301_sigma0.01.json`; with `DART_HORIZON_CONTROLS=10` (planning horizon 1.0 s instead of the archived 0.5 s, floors unchanged) `label_spread_s302_sigma0_h10.json`.
- `dart_rollout.py <planner_seed> <sigma> <noise_seed>`: one DART rollout (persistent planner, executed control = label + Gaussian noise of std sigma per dimension), storing every step (obs, label, executed control, next obs, qpos, qvel, targets, physics samples) and the strict outcome. `rollouts/p302_sigma0.01_n{1..4}.{npz,json}`.
- `consistency.py`: across the four rollouts of one planner seed, per step the pairwise L2 between labels, between observations and between the last-action part of the observation.
- `recover_from_policy.py <variant> <checkpoint> <b,...>`: the frozen policy's deterministic cold rollout, then for each b a fresh reset, the first b policy controls, and the planner from there. `recover_0099_online_3500.json` (the `0096` online_4000 run stopped at b = 2 because that policy's second control lies outside the planner's action search bounds, which the archived `action_search_bounds` refuses; its b = 1 row is in `recover_0096_online_4000.log`).

Native evaluator cache: `native_cache/` (copy of `control_period_0001/native_cache`). Nothing here is a demonstration, a checkpoint or primitive-eligible. Findings: `findings.md`.
