# Demonstration-initialized SAC from cold reset (template)

`prepare.py --name <stage> --demos mpc_cold_demonstrations_0001 [--bc 12000 --pretrain 2000 --online 4000 ...]` stamps `artifacts/<stage>/` with the frozen numerical source (the recovery stage snapshot), the environment config, the SAC0001 learner configuration (fresh networks), the hash-bound verified demonstrations, `protocol.json` and `execution_manifest.json`. `run.py --execute` then trains a fresh actor by mean-only BC on the demonstration rows (episode-weighted sampling), initializes the std, pretrains the critics with the archived Soft-SARSA step on demonstration rows, and runs online SAC from cold reset with one archived update per interaction on 30/70 demonstration/online batches. Cold deterministic and stochastic evaluations run at `bc_complete`, `pretrained` and every online checkpoint in separate environments with training RNG restored; nothing from evaluation enters replay; checkpoints preserve learner, replay and RNG state.

This is an artifact-level replica of the paper's BC -> critic -> online stages without the MPC scaffolding chunks, using demonstrations from the diagnostic prior rather than a SAC-derived primitive (disclosed deviation). Cold-reset success is the only deployment-relevant measure; nothing here is primitive-eligible.
