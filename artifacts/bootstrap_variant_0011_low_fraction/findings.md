# Online demonstration share .05: no frozen prefix-6 success, prefix-7 retention still lost, demonstration values drift further than at .3

**Dropping the online demonstration share from .3 to .05 (13 of 256 rows per batch, pretraining share unchanged) did not stabilise the actor.** The stage completed its full budget (500 mixed critic updates, 2,000 mixed SAC updates, exit 0, 568.5 s wall): no frozen-policy success at prefix 6 or cold at any checkpoint, zero of 162 completed online training episodes succeeded (SAC0004: 2 of 94), prefix-7 retention fell from 6/6 at baseline to 0/6 by online 500 and stayed there, and the critic's values on the eight demonstration rows drifted to bias about −.44 (MPC) and −.71 (recorded) by online 2,000, against −.09 / −.23 in SAC0004. Only prefix-8 retention survived: 6/6 at baseline and at 2,000, 2/6 at 500 (deterministic trial held 92 controls to the horizon without a turn; stochastic 2/5) and 5/6 at 1,000 (deterministic pass; stochastic 4/5).

## Interpretation

The hypothesis was that repeating the 8 rows about 9.6 times per online batch destabilises the actor. With repetition cut to about 1.6 times, the handoff std rose fifteenfold rather than threefold, the actor's deterministic handoff action moved further from both demonstration labels at online 500 and 1,000 than SAC0004 did at the same points, and the critic forgot the demonstration values it had learned in pretraining. By online 2,000 the actor was further from the MPC label than SAC0004 (1.23 versus .33) but closer to the recorded label (.79 versus 1.24); neither distance produced a prefix-6 success. Under this single seed, the demonstration share is not the destabilising factor; a smaller share leaves the 243 prefix-6 failure rows per batch to dominate the actor update sooner. Retention of prefix 7 is lost at online 500 exactly as with share .3, before any prefix-6 success exists. The late holding behaviour matches SAC0004's online-1,000 plateau near 5.6–5.9 rad: stable grasp, no turn.
