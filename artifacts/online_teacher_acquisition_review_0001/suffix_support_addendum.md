# Revised priority after the fixed-prefix support probe

Prioritize a bounded reverse-prefix curriculum using the original 60D bootstrap initialization. Keep the two-episode, 40-query online-MPC collection protocol in `review.md` as a fallback for states where shortening the prefix exposes missing policy support. This supersedes its priority as the immediate next experiment; it does not change its implementation requirements. This addendum is a read-only analysis of saved artifacts. No new physical episodes, teacher queries, fitting, or core changes were performed.

The same initial checkpoint (`66681b0f9651b21edb39ef94cb9827e8dcb1ab08249ac7e82010f81c114cf937`, BC 12000 / online 0) at latent standard deviation .05 has the following actual outcomes:

| 17043 | 4 | drop | 1 | 6.705910615 |

The deterministic assisted control reproduces the original nine-control success exactly. At the actual prefix-8 handoff, force-qualified progress is 5.20801449 radians: 1.07517 radians remain. The eight recorded controls, complete integration states, and 400 physics samples match the source in every assisted trial. All five stochastic suffixes genuinely terminate after their first policy-controlled action under the existing strict criteria. No post-success hold was tested. The six assisted episodes together took 1.5767 seconds in the saved driver; this is an observed collection time, not a learning-runtime estimate.

These results are compatible. The late state has useful local stochastic action support, while cold stochastic execution fails before reaching it. The earlier cold data measure reachability under repeated noisy feedback, not the action distribution conditioned on this later state. Conversely, five successful actions at a single exact handoff do not establish a basin of nearby supported states, support at prefixes 0–7, a reliable multi-step suffix, or cold-start robustness. The seeds are reused, but random sampling begins at the handoff, so the suffix uses the first draw from each seed rather than the ninth draw from an uninterrupted stochastic episode. This is a conditional-support comparison, not paired equal-noise propagation along the original path.

The successful checkpoint is the original **60D** bootstrap initialization. Its support result must not be attributed to the later **61D clock** actors. The clock actors, their 73/86-pair datasets, and the MPC recovery labels remain separate diagnostics. There is no need to import clock weights, append time, or repeat a head solve to test the proposed 60D curriculum.

Use physical cold replay of the immutable successful prefix as the assisted reset procedure. Preserve accumulated angle, previous action, contact-loss history, sticky no-palm state, previous joint/object measurements, MuJoCo integration state, and actual elapsed time. Do not replace the handoff with a qpos/qvel-only reset, clear task history, zero the elapsed clock, or restart the 100-control physical horizon at handoff. Prefix length must be explicit per episode; failure before handoff is a recorded prefix failure, not a skipped trial.

For a future bounded learning protocol, start at the already-supported prefix length 8 and reduce assistance only through a predeclared stage schedule or gate, ending at length 0. Allocate finite policy-transition and update budgets per stage, record every failed suffix, and keep a fixed cold-reset evaluation separate. Root should select those budgets before launching; the present support probe does not establish a particular learning rate, warm-up, gate threshold, or earlier prefix. Do not automatically launch a search across prefix lengths, noise scales, or learner settings on the strength of this review.

Archive the entire physically executed episode, but distinguish recorded-prefix transitions from policy suffix transitions. If the intended learner is trained only on suffix experience, build its replay from the actual handoff observation onward. The last actual prefix observation becomes the first suffix observation; its action, reward, successor, terminal flags, and density must all be from the actual policy step. For longer suffixes, `next_actions` must point to the next actually executed policy action. Never label a recorded prefix command as sampled policy behavior or assign it a fabricated log probability. An administrative budget stop preserves environmental flags, records collector truncation separately, and has no valid next action unless that action was actually executed.

Track physical controls, recorded-prefix controls, policy interactions, completed assisted episodes, cold-reset successes, and actual learner updates separately. In particular, eight prescribed controls must not silently count as eight policy interactions toward a 256-interaction learning-start threshold. At prefix 8 the observed suffix is one terminal transition per episode; the current warm-up would therefore require 256 such policy interactions before an online update if preserved. Any alternative warm-up is an explicit diagnostic choice, not an inferred consequence of the five successes. Assisted success never satisfies the frozen cold-policy primitive gate or the main-stage demonstration gate.

The terminal suffix targets are directly observed large positive rewards (approximately 228–230 in these traces). Their Bellman target is the actual terminal reward, without a bootstrap value or same-action entropy subtraction. Earlier frozen-critic fits used cold failure data; their negative target centering cannot be assumed appropriate for this new positive distribution. Record critic predictions and target errors on the real assisted returns, rather than interpreting the old cold-state Q calibration as evidence against local suffix support. This is a measurement requirement, not a request to change the critic or perform another fit here.

The online-MPC fallback remains useful when actual earlier policy-visited states cannot reach the supported suffix reliably. Query only at those visited states under a separately fixed budget. Preserve the distinction between counterfactual supervised teacher proposals and physically executed teacher transitions, and retain the CEM warm-start/reset and successor rules in `review.md`. Forty queries were estimated from 26 archived plans to cost roughly 3–6 minutes and could cover up to 40 visited decisions; the suffix probe already supplies positive physical policy experience far more cheaply, but at only one handoff state. Neither path guarantees learned cold-start success.
