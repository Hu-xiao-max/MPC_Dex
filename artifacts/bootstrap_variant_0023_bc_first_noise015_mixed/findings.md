## Interpretation and scope

The clean-suffix half of the hypothesis is roughly consistent with the data; the basin-widening half is not supported. Sigma .015 left the clean suffix largely intact (prefix 8 21–30/30 from 500 on and 24–30/30 from 1,000 on; prefix 7 22/30 at 2,000, though 10–14/30 with two deterministic failures at 1,250–1,500 and 11/30 at 250). The prefix-6 deterministic trial passes at 6 of 8 dense checkpoints, but at the three checkpoints wave 1 evaluated (500, 1,000, 2,000) it is drop / pass / pass, the same as 0004 and below 0007's 3/3, so the dense schedule does not show a higher pass rate than wave 1. The perturbed neighbourhood was not mastered: noisy prefix-7 never exceeded 2/5, perturbed prefix-7 training success was 21 % (0019 unperturbed 76 %), and prefix-6 large-sample rates move between 1/30 and 16/30 across adjacent checkpoints, as they do in the seed-matched wave-2 comparators 0019 (0–19/30) and 0015 (2–11/30), so the 2,000 value is a checkpoint, not a plateau. This fits the synthesis pattern for no-MPC first-row BC and adds one seed suggesting .015 costs less than .025 at 2,000 (16/30 vs 4/30 and 2/30); it does not show that noise widens the basin, and the no-noise comparator 0019 (19/30 at 1,750, 0/30 at 2,000) leaves the sign of the noise effect undecided at this seed.
