# Handoff noise on mixed prefixes: the only 5/5 stochastic prefix-6 point in wave 1, at online 1,000, not held at 2,000; cold unchanged

**Executing the last prefix control with N(0, .025²) noise on prefixes 7/8, with prefixes sampled from {6, 7, 8} and the three recorded demonstration rows only, produced a frozen prefix-6 peak that no other wave-1 variant reached:** the deterministic prefix-6 trial passes at online 500, 1,000 and 2,000, and the five stochastic seeds pass 5/5 at online 1,000 (other wave-1 variants top out at 3/5; variants 0002–0006 also have frozen prefix-6 passes, so this is not the first). The result is not stable: at 2,000 the stochastic prefix-6 trials fall to 1/5, prefix-7 retention oscillates (5/5 → 0/5 → 4/5 → 2/5), and the perturbed prefix-7 handoff the noise was meant to make robust ends at 0/5. All 9 cold trials fail (deterministic drops after 1, 3, 2 and 1 controls; 5/5 stochastic drops at control 1 at the final point). The stage completed its full budget (exit 0, 573.1 s). The seed replicate (variant 0018) has since completed and did not reproduce the online-1,000 peak.

## Interpretation and scope

The hypothesis was that a noisy neighbourhood around recorded control 7 would stop the stochastic policy's own deviation from capping prefix-6 success. The prefix-6 numbers support it at one point only: the online-1,000 policy sits .046 from the recorded control with std .10 and passes 6/6; by 2,000 the mean has moved to .090 and the std has nearly doubled to .20, and the stochastic passes drop to 1/5 while the deterministic trial still passes. The neighbourhood itself was not mastered: perturbed prefix-7 handoffs succeed 21% (32/150) in training and end 0/5 frozen, below the inherited 2/5; the perturbed prefix-8 handoff improves monotonically (2/5, 2/5, 3/5, 4/5) but never reaches 5/5, whereas the unperturbed prefix-8 trials (one control from the goal) pass 6/6 at every point. Compared with variant 0001 (mixed prefixes, no noise, MPC rows kept), which had zero prefix-6 frozen successes and lost prefix 7 at online 500 and after (deterministic drop, 0/5 stochastic at 500, 1,000 and 2,000), this variant changes two factors, so the noise cannot be isolated from MPC-row removal here.

Single seed here. The seed replicate `handoff_noise_mixed_r2` (variant 0018, train seed 19180, checkpoints every 250) has completed and does not reproduce the peak: at online 1,000 the prefix-6 deterministic trial drops and the stochastic trials pass 0/5; its only stochastic prefix-6 passes are 2/5 at online 1,250 (with a deterministic pass) and 1/5 at 2,000; cold 0/14. The online-1,000 5/5 point is therefore a single-seed observation. Frozen results are checkpoint samples, and the training rates are changing-policy episodes. All collected data are assisted or demonstration-mixed and ineligible for primitives; cold trials 0/9, so no checkpoint is deployable or a verified initialization.
