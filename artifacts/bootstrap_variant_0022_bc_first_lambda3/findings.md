## Interpretation and scope

The hypothesis (lambda 10 over-constrains the mean at the handoff) is not supported at this seed. With lambda 3 the mean still converged to within L2 .03–.07 of recorded control 7 (versus .02 at lambda 10), so the supervision was not loosened in a way that changed the first control; what changed is that the weaker pull coincided with a slower rise in training successes (4 % → 20 % per window, never the 38 % peak of 0004), a single deterministic pass that used 4 controls with only two loaded fingers, a 1/5 stochastic ceiling, and a prefix-7 loss at 1,750–2,000 (deterministic palm support at both, stochastic 2/5 then 0/5). Neither lambda-10 first-row run ended that way (0004 and 0017 both 6/6 at 2,000), although 0017's deterministic prefix-7 trial did drop once at 1,750 with stochastic 5/5 before recovering, so a single late deterministic prefix-7 failure is within what the lambda-10 replicate showed; the stochastic loss without recovery is what 0022 adds. The training count 66/435 is closer to the no-BC control 0002 (64/441) than to 0004 (109/432), consistent with the wave-1 attribution that first-row BC at lambda 10 was the best single factor for prefix-6 stochastic success and retention; lambda 3 behaves like a partial dose. The wave-1 synthesis puts seed-to-seed spread at about one deterministic pass and two of five stochastic trials per cell, so the prefix-6 differences alone are within noise; the late prefix-7 loss and the 43-success training gap are the larger signals, but a single seed cannot separate lambda from seed.
