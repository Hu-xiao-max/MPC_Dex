The bounded BC comparison produced no validated closed-loop full turn on the new 40 mm demonstration. Exact teacher replay succeeds at 2.2 s with 6.301309 force-qualified radians and bit-exact training observations. All six prespecified actor audits fail before completing a turn. Consequently there is no successful learned-policy episode eligible for the conditional two-second continuous-policy aftermath audit.

This uses the same settings as the earlier 45 mm diagnostic: the existing `SACAgent` 60/16 network with two 256-unit LayerNorm/tanh layers, two unused critics, seed 17001, Adam BC learning rate 3e-4, CUDA, one Torch CPU thread, and exactly 6,000 mean-only updates per epsilon. 241 of the 352 scalar teacher actions are exactly saturated (68.5%). Epsilon .02 changes those targets by .02 normalized units, up to .01883 rad in joint target space. Its 1,000- and 3,000-update actors already fit the clipped latent targets to approximately 1e-13 MSE, so their remaining command error is imposed by clipping. The 6,000-update .02 checkpoint moves away from that near-exact fit under continued fixed-rate Adam updates; every prespecified checkpoint is retained. Epsilon 1e-6 caps float32 latent targets at 7.24773 and lowers clipping-induced joint error to 9.54e-7 rad. It is an explicit diagnostic bootstrap setting, not the paper BC setting.

Direct teacher-command replay with clipping shows sensitivity independently of learned feedback. Epsilon .02 makes the teacher drop at .4 s after peak force-qualified progress 2.59384 rad. Epsilon 1e-6 makes it hit the palm at 1.1 s after peak progress 2.27209 rad. Exact replay remains successful. Re-observation of these same two fixed sequences reproduces their saved qpos traces exactly, establishing reproducibility within the frozen simulator and assets.

In the 1e-6-clipped teacher replay, the first command changes by at most 1.0133e-6 normalized units. After that first .1 s control, max qpos error is .00046064 and max qvel error .0167227. The first change in loaded contact-partner set, using the auditor's 1e-6 N threshold, occurs at **.014 s**; the first geometric contact-partner change occurs at **.110 s**, when the clipped run lacks a contact with the index-finger medial geom. Loaded finger membership first differs at **.122 s**. By .2 s, max qpos/qvel errors reach .0251156/5.30108. The stable final teacher grasp therefore does not imply a robust approach trajectory.

The best-fitting 6,000-update clone initially stays much closer than that clipped replay, then diverges through feedback. Its actual normalized action max error against the time-aligned teacher grows from 1.0133e-6 at control 1 to .0001988 at control 6, .0005788 at control 8, .0129168 at control 9, .0661990 at control 10, and .875321 at control 13. Its final force-qualified angle is 4.35114 rad after a peak of 4.94182. These errors are measured on actual visited observations, while the tiny teacher-fitting MSE is measured only on the recorded states.

The evidence supports improving expert trajectory robustness and coverage around visited states before treating BC as a reliable closed-loop bootstrap. Additional fitting steps on this one trace do not establish that capability. This does not show that broader data or a more robust successful trajectory could not work. No further settings were swept, no success threshold was weakened, and no post-failure or next actions were fabricated.
